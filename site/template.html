A FEW THINGS YOU MIGHT ASK

Simple answers.

Can I keep my domain?

Yes. We send instructions for your DNS provider. The exact records depend on your host. Until you connect it, your website runs on our subdomain.

What counts as a small update?

Text, hours, prices, adding or removing a service, and swapping photos you supply. Up to 5 requests per month, each requiring up to 30 minutes, completed within 2 business days.

Do I need to send photos or write the site?

We start with verified public facts and original illustrations. You confirm corrections before launch. Any photos, logos or testimonials you supply must be yours to use or used with permission.

Do you guarantee search rankings or leads?

No. This is a website, hosting and maintenance service. SEO campaigns and advertising are outside the plan.

Is there a contract or setup fee?

No setup fee and no long-term commitment. The subscription is $$119 a month. Cancel through your receipt email, effective at the end of the paid month.
