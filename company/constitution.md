# Constitution

Owner: founder; supervision under 30 minutes/day. Owner does not sell.
Goal: 10,000 SAR/month from a self-serve online service.
Flow: landing page → prospect-specific sample → payment link → email delivery.
No calls, demos, negotiation, WhatsApp, or automated DMs.
Claude plans, researches, proposes the product, and reviews. Codex builds and audits.
Owner approves spending and decisions escalated after two review rounds.
No spending, outbound prospect email, deployment, or provider runs are authorized by seed tasks.
Day 1–2: Markdown-in-Git, SQLite, cron, operating memory, watchdog only.
No sub-agents, customer code, orchestrator service, dashboards, or extra infrastructure.
Tasks require measurable success, at most three attempts and two review rounds.
All tasks must be created via scripts/watchdog.py --create PATH to enforce capacity.
Malformed memory is an operational error, never permission to proceed.
Never commit credentials. Keep report mail configuration in the execution environment.
